Good food has a way of bringing people back.

Kalpana Restaurant is a local Jorhat favourite for simple, satisfying vegetarian food. The menu brings together comforting Indian classics, breakfast staples and traditional sweets.

From a hearty plate of chole bhature to a cooling lassi or a sweet finish, the experience is designed to feel uncomplicated, generous and familiar.
